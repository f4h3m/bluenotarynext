Being prepared with compliant notarial certificate loose certificates and standardized document templates is essential for both mobile notaries and remote online signing agents. If a principal hands you a document lacking statutory notarial wording, having these standardized templates ready saves time and ensures legal validity.

## The Core 4 Notarial Certificate Templates

- **General Acknowledgment Certificate:** Used when the signer confirms they willingly signed a document and verified their identity.
- **Jurat with Oath/Affirmation:** Required when the signer swears or affirms under penalty of perjury that the contents of a statement are true.
- **Copy Certification Certificate:** Attests that a reproduced photocopy of an original document is a true, full, and accurate copy.
- **Signature by Mark / Credible Witness Form:** Used when a signer is physically unable to write their name or lacks standard government identification.

## Specialized Business and Real Estate Templates

- **Affidavit of Single Status / Non-Marriage:** Frequently required for international travel, foreign property purchases, or apostille authentication.
- **Vehicle Title Transfer Bill of Sale:** Standardizes vehicle asset transfers between private parties requiring notarization.
- **Parental Consent for Minor Travel:** Authorizes one parent or guardian to travel internationally with minor children.

## Digital Document Tagging Best Practices

When converting templates into online notarization sessions on **BlueNotary**:

- Place signature fields at least 15 pixels away from dividing lines to prevent clipping.
- Use explicit timestamp anchors so the execution date is automatically populated.
- Pre-save custom template bundles in your dashboard to launch client signings with a single click.

## Summary Checklist

Keep digital PDF copies and laminated hard copies in your signing binder. Regularly verify that statutory wording matches your state's most recent legislative updates.
