Remote Online Notarization (RON) has transformed legal document execution across Wyoming. Because transactions involve an audio-video call between the notary and the signers, strict state laws regulate how these audio-visual recordings are stored and preserved.

## Mandatory Retention Rules in Wyoming

Under Wyoming statutes, audio-visual recordings must be retained for a mandatory period (ranging from 5 to 10 years) to protect against title disputes or allegations of fraud.

## Authorized Access Rights

Access to session recordings is strictly restricted to:
- The signers and principal parties.
- The commissioned notary public.
- Subpoenas and judicial auditing authorities.
