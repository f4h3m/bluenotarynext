Under Missouri Revised Statutes Chapter 486 (the Missouri Electronic and Remote Notary Act), commissioned notaries public can register to perform both Electronic Notarizations (in-person electronic) and Remote Online Notarizations (RON).

## Prerequisites for Missouri Notaries

Before applying for remote authorization, ensure you meet the following baseline conditions:

- An active Missouri Notary Public commission in good standing.
- Completion of the Missouri Secretary of State's online training course and passing the examination.
- An active **$10,000 surety bond** covering electronic/remote notarizations.
- Selection of an approved electronic notary system provider (such as **BlueNotary**).

## Step 1: Complete the Secretary of State Training & Exam

Log into the Missouri Secretary of State Notary portal:

- Review the Missouri Notary Public Handbook and remote statutes.
- Complete the online training module.
- Pass the required 20-question multiple-choice exam with an 80% or higher score.

## Step 2: Obtain Your Missouri Digital Certificate & Seal

Missouri requires cryptographic integrity for all digital acts:

- **Digital Certificate:** Procure a digital signature from a recognized provider that links your unique cryptographic key to the document.
- **Missouri Electronic Seal:** Must contain your name as commissioned, your commission ID, the words *"Electronic Notary Public"*, *"State of Missouri"*, and your county of commission.

## Step 3: Register with the Secretary of State

Submit your electronic registration through the Secretary of State portal, providing proof of your bond amendment and technology provider agreement. Once approved, you will receive your electronic notary endorsement.

## Best Practices for Missouri Online Notaries

- Always verify that signers complete the requisite credential analysis and dynamic KBA prior to joining the video conference.
- Record every session and preserve your electronic journal entries for the mandatory statutory period.
- Ensure audio and video quality meet statutory clarity guidelines throughout the call.
