Executing legal contracts with multiple signers has historically been a logistical nightmare. Coordinating schedules, mailing documents back and forth via overnight courier, and finding physical notaries in different cities often leads to days or weeks of unnecessary delays.

Remote Online Notarization (RON) eliminates this friction through multi-signer digital sessions.

## 1. Commercial Lease Agreements

Multi-tenant commercial leases often require signatures from corporate officers, guarantors, and property managers spread across multiple offices.

## 2. Multi-Member LLC Operating Agreements

Founders and investors located in different states can join a single live video signing room, verify their identities simultaneously, and execute their partnership charter within minutes.

## 3. Real Estate Deeds of Conveyance (Warranty & Quitclaim Deeds)

When co-owners sell inherited real estate or couples divide property, all title holders must execute the deed before a notary. RON allows co-signers in different states to sign the same electronic instrument seamlessly.

## 4. Separation and Marital Settlement Agreements

Parties undergoing divorce often prefer not to meet face-to-face. With multi-party online notarization, each spouse can join the video conference independently with their attorneys.

## 5. Family Trust Restatements and Amendments

Trust amendments often require consents from multiple co-trustees and current beneficiaries.

## 6. Corporate Shareholder Resolutions and Buy-Sell Contracts

Annual board actions and buy-sell agreements involving dispersed executive shareholders can be executed on-demand.

## 7. Multi-Party Settlement and Release Agreements

Civil litigation settlements involving multiple claimants, insurers, and defense counsel can finalize releases in a single recorded session.

## 8. Oil, Gas, and Mineral Lease Rights

Mineral rights owners across the country can execute royalty agreements without postal turnaround bottlenecks.

## How BlueNotary Orchestrates Multi-Signer Sessions

- **Simultaneous Verification:** Each party completes automated ID validation and KBA independently before the call.
- **Unified Video Signing Room:** All participants join a recorded conference where the notary guides document execution.
- **Immediate PDF Distribution:** Completed, tamper-evident sealed copies are emailed instantly to all signatories upon conclusion.
