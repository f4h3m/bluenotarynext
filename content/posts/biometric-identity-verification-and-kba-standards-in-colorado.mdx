Identity proofing is the foundational security layer of remote online notarization in Colorado.

## Multi-Layered Authentication Protocol

1. **Credential Analysis:** Automated scanning of security watermarks and microprints on government IDs.
2. **3D Facial Liveness Detection:** Verifying live human presence against the ID photo.
3. **Dynamic KBA Questions:** 5 time-limited identity questions sourced from credit history.

## Fraud Prevention Impact

This multi-factor approach stops synthetic identity fraud, stolen documents, and impersonation attempts.
