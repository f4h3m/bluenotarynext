Creating an estate plan no longer requires thousands of dollars in attorney retainer fees for individuals with standard asset profiles. Modern digital estate planning platforms provide guided questionnaires, state-specific legal forms, and seamless remote online notarization.

In this guide, we compare the three leading online estate planning solutions: **Will and Trust Maker (Nolo/Quicken)**, **Trust & Will**, and **FreeWill**.

## Quick Comparison Table

- **Trust & Will:** Best for modern, design-forward user experience with integrated electronic deed transfers and live attorney support add-ons.
- **Will and Trust Maker:** Best for power users seeking extensive legal customizability across standalone software.
- **FreeWill:** Best for basic, cost-free charitable bequests and straightforward testamentary wills.

## 1. Trust & Will: Features and Experience

Trust & Will is widely recognized for its polished, modern interface and state-specific compliance updates.

- **Strengths:** Intuitive questionnaire design, easy annual updates, and direct integration with remote online notary platforms.
- **Drawbacks:** Requires an ongoing annual membership for document edits after the first year.
- **Pricing:** Individual wills from $199; revocable living trust packages from $599.

## 2. Quicken WillMaker & Trust: Features and Experience

Quicken WillMaker by Nolo is one of the oldest and most legally comprehensive DIY estate planning packages on the market.

- **Strengths:** One-time purchase includes over 35 legal documents (healthcare directives, executor checklists, financial POAs).
- **Drawbacks:** Interface feels more traditional and software-heavy compared to modern cloud SaaS tools.
- **Pricing:** $99 to $209 one-time software license.

## 3. FreeWill: Features and Experience

FreeWill partners with non-profits and charitable foundations to offer 100% free estate planning tools to the public.

- **Strengths:** Truly free of charge with simple questionnaire steps.
- **Drawbacks:** Limited customizability for complex asset structures, blended families, or business succession clauses.
- **Pricing:** Completely Free.

## Final Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

- Choose **Trust & Will** if you want a seamless, all-digital online workflow with cloud storage.
- Choose **WillMaker** if you prefer a one-time purchase with comprehensive legal document variations.
- Choose **FreeWill** if your estate is simple and you wish to incorporate charitable giving.
