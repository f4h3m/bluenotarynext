Using exact statutory notarial certificate wording is mandatory for legal recognition in Virginia.

## Acknowledgments vs. Jurats

- **Acknowledgment:** Confirms the signer willingly executed the document and verified their identity.
- **Jurat:** Requires the signer to swear or affirm under penalty of perjury that document contents are true.

## Digital Certificate Placement Rules

Ensure electronic notary stamps do not overlap text or signatures while clearly displaying commission expiration dates and state seal boundaries.
