Starting an online notary business has never been more accessible. With modern cloud platforms and digital seal solutions, you can serve clients nationwide right from your home office.

## Step 1: Check State Requirements
Ensure you hold an active notary commission in your state and apply for RON authorization with your Secretary of State.

## Step 2: Acquire Approved Digital Tools
You will need an X.509 compliant digital certificate, an electronic seal, and an account with an approved RON provider.
