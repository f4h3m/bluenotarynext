Having standardized, state-compliant certificate templates ensures notary publics in Maine can handle any signing assignment without friction.

## Standard Loose Certificates

- Individual and Corporate Acknowledgment Forms.
- Jurats with Oath and Affirmation Wording.
- Copy Certification Certificates.
- Signature by Mark and Credible Witness Affidavits.
