Digital estate planning enables residents in Mississippi to execute legally binding wills and powers of attorney without wet ink signatures.

## Statutory Requirements for Valid Electronic Wills

- The testator must demonstrate full testamentary capacity.
- Two independent witnesses must observe the live video session.
- An authorized notary attaches a self-proving electronic certificate.

## Reducing Probate Disputes

Timestamped video archives and multi-factor identity authentication provide indisputable evidence against undue influence or capacity challenges.
