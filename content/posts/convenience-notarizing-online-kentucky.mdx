The Commonwealth of Kentucky enacted permanent Remote Online Notarization (RON) legislation under KRS Chapter 423. Kentucky businesses, legal professionals, and residents now enjoy unprecedented convenience when executing critical affidavits, real estate deeds, and commercial agreements.

## Why Kentucky Residents Choose Online Notarization

Traditional notarization required taking time off work, traveling to a bank or notary office, and coordinating with signers in person. With online notarization, Kentucky signers can complete their transactions in 10 minutes or less from their smartphone, tablet, or laptop.

- **Anywhere Availability:** Connect with a commissioned Kentucky notary whether you are in Louisville, Lexington, or traveling out of state.
- **Round-the-Clock Flexibility:** Schedule signing appointments in the evening or on weekends when standard offices are closed.
- **Enhanced Security:** Automated government ID credential scanning and knowledge-based authentication provide far greater fraud protection than manual visual inspections.

## Key Legal Documents Commonly Notarized in Kentucky

- **Real Estate Deeds and Mortgages:** Complete home purchases, refinancing packages, and seller documents without physical settlement meetings.
- **Estate Planning Instruments:** Notarize healthcare surrogacies, durable powers of attorney, and trusts from home.
- **Automotive and DMV Forms:** Complete Kentucky title transfer affidavits and vehicle bill of sales electronically.
- **Corporate Agreements:** Authorize multi-member LLC resolutions, commercial contracts, and vendor agreements.
