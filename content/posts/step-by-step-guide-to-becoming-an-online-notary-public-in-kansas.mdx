Expanding your credentials to include Remote Online Notarization in Kansas allows you to serve clients nationwide from the comfort of your home office.

## Prerequisites and Commissioning Steps in Kansas

1. Hold an active traditional notary commission in good standing.
2. Complete state-mandated training and pass required examinations.
3. Secure a surety bond and E&O insurance policy.
4. Acquire an X.509 digital certificate and state-compliant electronic seal.
