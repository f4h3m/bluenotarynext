Florida was one of the earliest adopters of comprehensive Remote Online Notarization (RON) laws under Chapter 117 of the Florida Statutes. As a Florida commissioned notary, expanding your credentials to include online notarization allows you to serve clients across Florida and internationally.

## Requirements to Qualify as a Florida RON Notary

To apply for an online notary registration in Florida, you must fulfill these prerequisites:

- Hold an active traditional Florida Notary Public commission or Civil-Law Notary appointment.
- Complete a state-approved 2-hour Florida Remote Online Notary education course.
- Secure a **$25,000 Notary Public Bond** and a **$25,000 Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insurance Policy**.
- Contract with a state-compliant Florida RON technology provider like **BlueNotary**.

## Step 1: Complete the State-Approved Training Course

Florida requires all prospective online notaries to complete an accredited education program covering:

- Credential analysis and knowledge-based authentication rules.
- Florida electronic journal and 10-year audio-video retention mandates.
- Supervised electronic signing procedures and statutory certificate wording.

## Step 2: Acquire Your Digital Seal and X.509 Certificate

You will need state-compliant digital tools:

- **X.509 Compliant Digital Certificate:** Issued by an approved Certificate Authority (IdentTrust, DigiCert).
- **Florida Digital Seal:** Contains your name, commission number, commission expiration date, and the words *"State of Florida Online Notary Public"*.

## Step 3: Submit Your Application to the Florida Department of State

1. Complete the *Application for Registration as an Online Notary Public* (Form DOC-NOT-1).
2. Attach your $25,000 bond rider, training certificate, and technology provider declaration.
3. Submit the required $10 state filing fee to the Department of State Division of Corporations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Florida online notaries notarize real estate closings?
Yes, Florida is a major hub for digital real estate closings, and remote online notarization is accepted by all major underwriters for Florida property transactions.
